# Decision Log

Non-obvious decisions made while building this pipeline, with the real numbers/specifics behind each.

- **Brand selection favored reply substance over raw volume.** comcastcares (33,031 tweets) was chosen over AmericanAir (36,764 tweets — the actual volume leader) and British_Airways (29,361) because early thread sampling showed Comcast's replies looked more troubleshooting/billing-substantive rather than pure DM-deflection (`reports/brand_exploration.md`). In hindsight this looks weaker than it did at the time: Phase 1/5 later found 71.4% of even the *kept* Comcast threads still defer to a DM ask.

- **Pure-deflection threads were dropped and logged separately, not kept as grounding data.** 197 of 32,921 candidate threads matched a "DM-ask with fewer than 4 remaining content words after stripping boilerplate" heuristic and were excluded from `data/comcastcares_threads.jsonl`, with the reason logged per-thread to `reports/dropped_deflections.jsonl` rather than silently discarded.

- **Truncated-fragment threads (4.2%) were knowingly left in, not heuristically filtered.** 1,385 of 32,724 kept threads are truncated multi-tweet reply fragments (e.g. a kept reply reading only "address, and phone number so I can assist further with this issue. -KW", missing its first half). A "starts with a lowercase word" heuristic can catch these, but can't reliably distinguish a real fragment from a legitimately terse complete reply, so it was left as a documented limitation instead of a filter that would risk dropping valid short replies.

- **Model switch after mid-project deprecation.** `llama-3.3-70b-versatile`, the model Phase 2 was originally written against, no longer exists on Groq. Switched to `openai/gpt-oss-20b` with `reasoning_effort="low"` specifically because `gpt-oss-120b` (and the `compound`/`compound-mini` models, which route through it) shares an 8,000-token/minute free-tier budget that 50-message batches exceeded immediately, while `gpt-oss-20b` has its own separate 8,000 TPM bucket.

- **Taxonomy consolidation was restructured into map-reduce.** One-shot consolidation of all ~338 candidate intent labels (from 30 open-coding batches) would have needed more tokens than the 8,000 TPM budget in a single call. Phase 2's Stage B instead folds candidates into a running taxonomy in chunks of 40, then narrows the resulting ~15 entries into the final 10 in one last call.

- **Classification failures were reported transparently, not folded into "Other."** 101 of 1,500 sampled messages (6.7%) got no usable classification in Phase 2's anchoring pass due to malformed/truncated LLM JSON. These are called out as failures with an explicit note in `reports/intent_taxonomy.md`, rather than silently dropped or lumped into the "Other" bucket, since conflating "the model didn't answer" with "the model judged this ambiguous" would misrepresent both numbers.

- **Golden-set sampling blended proportional and rare-intent oversampling, plus deliberate hard cases.** `src/label_golden_set.py` allocates 170 of 200 candidate slots using 70% proportional / 30% uniform share per intent (so rare intents aren't reduced to 1-2 examples), plus 30 deliberately hard cases split 21/9 between the "Other" bucket (252 available) and outright classification failures (101 available). Pure random sampling would have been ~85% easy, well-separated examples and barely represented intents like Equipment Issues (4.1% share).

- **A degenerate-repetition bug in the main classifier was caught and fixed before trusting the eval.** The first real run scored 31.3% — worse than the 42.0% keyword baseline — because `gpt-oss-20b` at `temperature=0` with 50-message batches sometimes collapsed into repeating one label (one batch repeated "Escalation & Security" ~30 times straight: valid JSON, correct length, not a real classification). Fixed by shrinking batches to 25, adding a `looks_degenerate()` check (>50% single-label share, since no real intent exceeds ~20%), and retrying once at `temperature=0.4` before falling back to the majority label — raising accuracy to the reported 56.0%.

- **An escalation-reason templating bug was caught before finalizing Phase 5.** `decide_escalation()` originally printed a fixed string ("...low-risk intent...") on every auto-handle decision, which was factually wrong for auto-handled Escalation & Security examples (threads `1840835_1840837`, `1069001_1068999`) — cases auto-handled *despite* being a risky intent, because confidence/grounding outweighed it, not because they were low-risk. Fixed so the stated reason always reflects the actual contributing factors.

- **The LLM judge sample was reduced from 35 to 20 mid-run.** Repeated 429 rate-limit errors — even with 2.5s inter-call pacing and per-call retry/backoff — made judging all 35 sample outputs impractical within the timeline. The remaining 15 were left genuinely unattempted (not scored-and-failed), and `reports/judge_scores.md` states this explicitly rather than presenting 20 as if it were the full intended sample.

- **Human-judge agreement was reported even though it undercuts the judge's own headline number.** A blind human re-score of a 15-example overlap shows the judge agrees with a human 67% exactly / 93% within one point on "grounded" and 47%/93% on "tone," but only 7% exactly / 20% within one point on "addresses the actual issue" (`reports/judge_agreement.md`). Decided to foreground this divergence rather than only citing the judge's own 3.95/5 average on that dimension (`reports/judge_scores.md`), since the agreement numbers show that average is close to noise.

- **Two hallucinations were found only through manual reading, not any automated check.** Thread `2864317_2864315`'s draft invented a specific product, "Xfinity IR Extender (model #XG-IR-EXT)," and thread `2962208_2962207`'s draft invented a contact number, "1-800-CONFORT" — neither present in any retrieved grounding example. Both scored well on the automated judge's grounded/tone dimensions, meaning nothing in this pipeline would have caught them without a human reading the actual output.

- **TF-IDF retrieval was shipped with no real relevance floor, and that's flagged as a known gap.** `retrieve()` in `src/generate_reply.py` only filters candidates at cosine similarity > 0, so a misclassification cascades directly into wrong grounding. Thread `1380678_1380677` (a billing-hold complaint misclassified as "Service Availability & Scheduling") retrieved three technician-scheduling examples and drafted a reply promising to "get a tech scheduled ASAP" — unrelated to the customer's actual billing hold.

- **The 32/35 auto-handle rate was flagged as possibly too lenient rather than presented as a success metric.** `decide_escalation()` gates mainly on classifier self-reported confidence and whether *any* grounding examples were found — not their relevance — plus a modest bump for two intents (Billing Issues, Escalation & Security). It doesn't account for hallucination risk or retrieval relevance, both of which the failure analysis shows can coexist with "high confidence + grounding found" (the billing-hold and IR-extender cases both auto-handled).
